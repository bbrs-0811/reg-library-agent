Regulation / Rule Reference Description

Licence Conditions — TC000078

Issued 18 March 2025  ·  Supersedes version of 25 January 2025  ·  Signed: Lim Tuang Lee, Assistant Managing Director, Capital Markets Group

Trust Companies Act  ·  10 conditions attached to Phillip Trustee Pte. Ltd.'s trust business licence
No. Subject Condition
1Conflicts of InterestConduct business to avoid conflicts of interest; where conflicts arise, resolve them fairly and equitably.
2Professional Indemnity InsuranceImmediately inform MAS of any claim against PII cover. If claim ≥10% of aggregate coverage, furnish new PII within one month. Must maintain minimum PII coverage per TCA-G02.
3Independent AuditProduce books to independent auditors selected by MAS. All audit expenses borne by the licensee.
4Letter of Responsibility / UndertakingProvide MAS with Letter of Responsibility and Letter of Undertaking in such form as required. Ensure these remain in force for the duration of the licence.
5AML/CFT Inspection AccessPermit MAS to inspect books from time to time for monitoring compliance with AML/CFT directions, regulations and MAS Notices (under s.27B MAS Act).
6AML/CFT Information FurnishingFurnish MAS with any information required for monitoring compliance with AML/CFT measures and MAS Notices.
7Minimum PII CoverageMaintain minimum professional indemnity insurance coverage as set out in the Guidelines on Criteria for the Grant of a Trust Business Licence (TCA-G02).
8Foreign Jurisdiction ComplianceSatisfy compliance with all relevant laws in foreign jurisdictions before offering products/services to investors residing there.
9Foreign Law ComplianceLicensee and staff shall at all times comply with all foreign laws and regulations applicable to their activities.
10Immediate Notification TriggersImmediately notify MAS upon becoming aware of: (a) material adverse development affecting financial soundness, reputation or ability to carry on trust business; (b) resident manager/director no longer fit and proper; (c) controller/substantial shareholder no longer fit and proper; (d) likely inability to conduct business prudently due to controller influence.
